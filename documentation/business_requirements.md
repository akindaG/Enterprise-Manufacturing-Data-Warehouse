# Business Requirements Document

## Project
Enterprise Manufacturing Analytics Data Warehouse

## Business Domain
Manufacturing Industry

## Selected Business Process
Production Operations Analytics

## Business Scenario
An enterprise manufacturing organization produces multiple products across multiple factories. Operational systems capture daily production activities, but management requires historical analytical insights for decision making.

## Business Problems
The current operational environment makes it difficult to analyze:
- Production trends over time
- Factory performance
- Machine productivity
- Production costs
- Product output
- Defect patterns

## Data Warehouse Objectives
The Data Warehouse will provide a centralized analytical platform for production analysis and support business decision making.

## Analytical Requirements

### Production Analysis
- Total production quantity by month and year
- Production trends over time
- Highest volume products

### Factory Analysis
- Compare production output between factories
- Analyze factory performance over time

### Machine Analysis
- Identify high performing machines
- Analyze machine-related production efficiency

### Cost Analysis
- Analyze production cost by product and factory

### Quality Analysis
- Analyze defect counts and production quality trends
